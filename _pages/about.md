I am an Assistant Professor of Strategic Management at the Pamplin College of Business, Virginia Tech. My research examines how firms innovate — with a focus on knowledge networks, alliances, and organizational learning, often using large-scale data and network analysis.

My work has appeared in leading management journals, and I have taught Managerial Analytics, Strategic Management and International Business at the undergraduate levels.
