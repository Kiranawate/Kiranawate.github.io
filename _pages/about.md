I am an Assistant Professor of Strategic Management at the Pamplin College of Business, Virginia Tech. 

Knowledge is the great engine of innovation, and also its great escape. Artificial intelligence has made this plain on a planetary scale: frontier models trained at staggering cost in one country surface in another within months, carried by papers, people, and the models themselves. What AI reveals so vividly, my research finds everywhere: ideas gain value by being shared, with partners, with talent, with the wider web of minds that turn fragments into breakthroughs. Yet every act of sharing is an act of spillover, and what a firm teaches the world, the world soon teaches its rivals. I study how knowledge moves through networks of firms and inventors, why the channels built to spread it can never quite be closed, and what it means to compete when your best ideas refuse to stay home.

I teach Managerial Analytics, Strategic Management, and International Business.
